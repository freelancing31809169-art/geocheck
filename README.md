## ✨ What Does geocheck Do?

geocheck is designed to answer two simple questions:

### 📍 Where Does the Internet Think You Are?

Every time you visit a website, that site can estimate your physical location based on your IP address. This isn't always accurate - you might be using a VPN, your ISP might route traffic through different servers, or you might be traveling. geocheck shows you exactly what location data websites see when you connect.

### ⚡ How Directly Are You Connecting?

Your internet connection might not be as direct as you think. Data packets can hop through multiple servers across different cities or even countries before reaching their destination. geocheck measures and displays the directness of your connection, helping you understand:
- Whether your traffic is being routed efficiently
- If there are unexpected detours in your connection path
- How many "hops" your data takes to reach its destination

## 📊 Understanding Your Results Better

### What Does "Directness" Mean?

Your internet data travels through multiple points (called "hops") between your device and the websites you visit. A direct connection means your data takes the shortest path possible. Indirect connections might travel through different cities or countries, which can slow things down.

geocheck calculates a directness percentage:
- **90-100%**: Excellent - your connection is very direct
- **70-89%**: Good - minor detours but generally efficient
- **50-69%**: Fair - noticeable routing detours
- **Below 50%**: Poor - your data is taking a long path

### Why Does Location Matter?

Websites use your location to:
- Show relevant content and advertisements
- Apply regional pricing
- Follow local laws and regulations
- Provide localized versions of their services

Knowing what location websites see helps you understand why you might see different content than expected.
